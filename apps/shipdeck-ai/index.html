About us

ShipDeck AI is built and operated by gzx, an independent developer. We make it for people who ship apps on their own and want one calm place to see what needs attention today.

The app reads your data directly from Apple’s official App Store Connect API using an API key that you create and keep in the macOS Keychain. There is no ShipDeck AI server and no account to register: your numbers go from Apple to your Mac and stay there.

What it does

Revenue and subscription reports, weekly summaries, review and release status, phased release control, metadata editing, TestFlight builds and feedback, customer reviews with reply drafts, stability and performance metrics, and public-data competitor research. Optional AI assistants help explain reports and draft text using the provider and key you choose.
