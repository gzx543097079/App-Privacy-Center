Privacy Policy

Effective: November 5, 2026Operator: gzx

ShipDeck AI is local-first. We operate no server for the app, collect no analytics, show no ads, and do not track you. Your App Store Connect data goes from Apple to your Mac and stays there.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how ShipDeck AI (the “App”), operated by gzx, handles information when you use the App on your Mac.

2. Information stored on your Mac

The App stores your settings, cached reports, drafts, notification preferences, and analysis history in its own sandboxed container on your Mac. The App Store Connect API private key (.p8) that you import, and any AI or webhook keys that you enter, are stored in the macOS Keychain. Issuer ID and Key ID are stored on your Mac. None of this is sent to us.

3. Apple’s App Store Connect API

When you add an account, the App signs requests with your key and calls Apple’s App Store Connect API (api.appstoreconnect.apple.com) directly from your Mac to read your sales, subscription, review, build, TestFlight and similar data. Actions that change data (for example, publishing a version, saving metadata, or replying to a review) run only after you confirm them. Apple’s handling of this data is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

4. Public data lookups

The App may query public Apple endpoints (iTunes Search and Lookup) for competitor research, sending only the search terms, storefront, or app identifiers needed, and a public exchange-rate service (open.er-api.com) to convert currencies. These requests do not include your App Store Connect data or personal information.

5. Optional AI assistants

AI features are off until you enable them and provide your own API key. When you ask for an explanation, summary, draft, or analysis, the App sends the relevant text (for example, summarized figures, app status, review text, or a rejection letter you pasted) to the provider you selected, such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, Moonshot, Zhipu, Alibaba Cloud, OpenRouter, a local gateway, or a custom endpoint that you configured. The provider processes the data under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive this data. The App tells you what will be sent before it is sent.

6. Optional group notifications

If you configure a webhook (such as WeCom, DingTalk, Feishu, Slack, Discord, Telegram, or a custom URL), the App sends short status messages to that address when events you chose occur. The destination service handles those messages under its own policies.

7. Purchases

The 7-day trial and the one-time purchase are processed by Apple using StoreKit. We do not receive your payment information or Apple ID. The App reads your purchase status from Apple on your device to unlock features.

8. Notifications

The App can show local system notifications (for example, a review status change or a new review). These are generated on your Mac and are not sent through any server we operate.

9. What we do not do

We do not collect analytics or crash reports, do not display advertising, do not use advertising identifiers, do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and do not sell or share your information. If you contact support, we receive the information you include in your message so that we can reply.

10. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your Mac until you remove it. You can remove an account and its keys in the App’s settings, and deleting the App removes its container. Keychain items can be removed in Keychain Access. You can revoke the App Store Connect API key at any time in App Store Connect.

11. Children’s privacy

ShipDeck AI is a developer tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted here with a revised effective date.
