Common questions

How do I connect my account?

In App Store Connect open Users and Access → Integrations → App Store Connect API and create a team key. The Admin role unlocks every feature; for read-only use, choose Finance together with App Manager. Download the .p8 file (Apple lets you download it once), then enter the Issuer ID, Key ID and the .p8 file in the App.

Where is my key stored?

In the macOS Keychain on your Mac. It is never sent to us.

Why are some pages hidden?

The App checks what your key is allowed to do. Pages that need a permission your key does not have are hidden. Create a key with a broader role to see them.

Why is the App locked?

The App unlocks with the free 7-day trial or the one-time purchase. If you already purchased, open Settings and choose Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Why is today’s data missing?

Apple publishes reports on its own schedule: daily sales reports about a day later, monthly reports a few days after month end, and analytics reports about once a day. The page shows when the next update is expected.

How do I delete my data?

Remove the account in Settings, then delete the App. Keychain items can also be removed in Keychain Access. You can revoke the API key in App Store Connect at any time.
