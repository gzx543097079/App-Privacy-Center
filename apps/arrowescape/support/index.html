Arrow Escape

Support

Need help with a level, saved progress, privacy choice, or an ad? Email us and include the App version and a short description.

Gameplay and saved progress

Tap an arrow to remove it when its route is clear. Use Hint, Undo, Restart, and Fit in the game screen. Puzzle progress is saved on your device; it cannot be restored after deleting the App unless your device backup restores its data.

Privacy choices

Open Settings in the App to manage Analytics, Crash Reports, and available advertising Privacy Choices. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Report an ad

Use Settings → Report an Ad to prepare an email. Describe the ad, when it appeared, and why you are reporting it. Screenshots are optional; please avoid including sensitive personal information.
