Common questions

Which iPhones are supported?

DeskSnap runs on iOS 16 or later.

Are my scans uploaded?

No. Pages and PDFs stay on this iPhone. There is no account and no server we operate.

Why is there a watermark?

Free exports include a DeskSnap watermark. Pro removes it and unlocks extra pages, OCR, folders, and a library lock.

How do I unlock the library?

If you turned on the library lock, use Face ID or your device passcode. You can turn the lock off in Settings.

Does the App require an account?

No. DeskSnap does not require registration or login.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the App → Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → DeskSnap → Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the period ends.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
