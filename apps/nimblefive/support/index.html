Common questions

Does NimbleFive require an account?

No. NimbleFive does not require registration or login.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Core puzzles work offline. Advertisements, privacy messages, and external support links require an internet connection.

Where is my progress stored?

Progress is stored locally on your device. There is no cloud synchronization. Removing the App or clearing its data may permanently remove your scores, streaks, themes, and Sparks.

Why is an advertisement unavailable?

An advertisement may be unavailable because of connectivity, inventory, privacy choices, or a temporary provider issue. You can continue playing without an advertisement.

How do I change privacy choices?

Open NimbleFive, go to Settings, and select Privacy Choices. Options are shown when required and available for your region.

How do I report an advertisement?

Use the advertisement’s AdChoices or reporting control when available. You may also email support with the advertiser name, the approximate time it appeared, and a description. Do not include sensitive personal information.
