Gameplay and progress

Choose a chapter on the home screen, then tap a source bottle and a destination bottle to pour matching colors. Your completed levels and unlocked bottle and cap styles are saved on this device. Progress does not sync between devices and may be lost if the App is removed.

Language and appearance

Open Settings to change language, light or dark appearance, and reduced motion. Appearance styles unlock as you finish levels and can be selected in the collection.

Privacy and advertising

Open Settings to change the optional usage-and-crash-data choice. Ad privacy options appear when UMP reports that a form is required and available. Production ads are currently off while the consent message is being configured. Read the Privacy Policy and Advertising information.

Contact

For a level issue, a privacy question, or an ad report, email gengzhixiang1992@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, App version, and the level number when relevant. Do not include passwords or payment details.
