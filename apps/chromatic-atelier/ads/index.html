Advertising information

Updated: October 13, 2026Operator: gzx

The European consent message is published. Production ads are currently disabled in normal gameplay. The game remains playable without ads.

Advertising provider

The App integrates Google AdMob. Its registered iOS bundle identifier is com.nuvora.sortatelier. The account’s authorized seller record is published at app-ads.txt. This record identifies the seller; it does not mean an ad is shown on every screen.

Formats prepared for testing

Banner, interstitial, rewarded, native, and app-open formats have been tested in an iOS simulator using Google’s official demo ad units. A test ad is clearly labeled “Test mode.” These formats are not currently requested from production ad units during normal gameplay. Rewarded ads, if later offered, will be optional and must give the stated in-game reward only after the ad completion callback.

Privacy choices

The App uses Google UMP to refresh advertising consent information. Production ad requests stay disabled when the required status cannot be obtained. Where required, users will be able to review and change ad choices using the privacy-options entry in Settings. Read our Privacy Policy for categories of data Google and its partners may process once production ads are enabled.

Questions or ad reports

Email gengzhixiang1992@gmail.com. If reporting an ad, include the time, country, and a screenshot if you are comfortable sharing it. Do not send account passwords or payment information.
