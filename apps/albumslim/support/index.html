Common questions

Which iPhones are supported?

AlbumSlim runs on iOS 16 or later.

Where do deleted photos go?

Deletes go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app (usually 30 days). Open Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted to recover them.

Does AlbumSlim upload my photos?

No. Scanning stays on this iPhone. There is no account and no server we operate.

Why do I still need Pro?

Scanning and preview stay free. Pro unlocks unlimited batch cleanup. Free includes 5 deletes per day.

Does the App require an account?

No. AlbumSlim does not require registration or login.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the App → Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → AlbumSlim → Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the period ends.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
