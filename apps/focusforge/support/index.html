Common questions

Which iPhones are supported?

FocusForge runs on iOS 16 or later. Live Activities need iOS 16.1 or later on a supported device.

Is the timer free?

Yes. Start Focus is never behind a paywall. Pro unlocks Live Activities, history beyond 7 days, extra presets, focus noise, and CSV export.

I turned off notifications.

That is fine. The timer still runs. Notifications are optional.

Does FocusForge treat ADHD or anxiety?

No. FocusForge is a timer. It does not provide medical advice.

Does the App require an account?

No. FocusForge does not require registration or login.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the App → Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → FocusForge → Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the period ends.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
