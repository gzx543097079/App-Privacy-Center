Common questions

Which iPhones are supported?

QuickFrame runs on iOS 16 or later.

Does QuickFrame upload codes I scan?

No. Scanning and history stay on this iPhone. There is no account and no server we operate.

A link looks suspicious.

QuickFrame shows the decoded text before you open it. Opening a website leaves the App and uses Safari.

Why do I still need Pro?

Basic scans stay free. Pro unlocks unlimited history, batch scan, CSV export, and extra QR styles. Free keeps the last 10 scans.

Does the App require an account?

No. QuickFrame does not require registration or login.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the App → Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → QuickFrame → Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the period ends.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
