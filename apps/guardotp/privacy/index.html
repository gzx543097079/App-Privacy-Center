Privacy Policy

Effective: October 28, 2026Operator: Zhixiang Geng (耿志向)

GuardOTP Authenticator works without an account. Core features run on your device. We do not operate servers that store your content, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how GuardOTP Authenticator (the “App”), operated by Zhixiang Geng (耿志向), handles information when you use the App. The App does not require an account.

2. Camera

The App uses the camera only to scan authenticator setup QR codes (otpauth). Secrets are stored in the iPhone Keychain, not on a server we operate.

3. Information stored on your device

The App stores account labels, icons, folders, and settings. Secrets are stored in the iPhone Keychain on your device. This information stays there until you delete it in the App or delete the App.

Keep recovery codes from each service in a place you control. If you lose this iPhone without those codes, GuardOTP cannot restore the secrets.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location. We do not require login credentials. We do not display advertising. We do not use Firebase, advertising identifiers, or other analytics SDKs. If you email support, we receive only what you include in the message so we can reply.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. We do not receive your payment card details. The App receives transaction status from Apple so it can unlock paid features. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

6. Sharing

Files you export leave the device only when you choose a destination in the iOS share sheet. The recipient’s handling of that file is governed by that service.

7. Retention and deletion

Information stored on your device remains until you delete it or delete the App. Support email is kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

8. International processing

Apple may process purchase information in countries other than where you live, under Apple’s own privacy policy. We do not operate additional processors for App content.

9. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your rights

Because your content is stored on your device, you can view and delete it directly. For other requests, contact us at the address below.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the App or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
